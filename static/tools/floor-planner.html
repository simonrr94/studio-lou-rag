Click a room to select it, drag to move it, drag a corner to resize. For rooms that aren't rectangles, drag a + on a side to add a corner. To remove a corner, tap it and then the × beside it. With no corner picked, Delete removes the room.

Clear the plan

Paint over furniture drawn on the plan to clear the floor. Walls stay put, and your rooms and scale don't change.

Clearing a room also clears its printed name and dimensions; paint them back with Restore if you want them. Pinch or use the zoom buttons for small areas. Undo steps back one stroke at a time.

The walls on this plan weren't found when it was read, so the eraser can't protect them here. Go carefully near walls.

Use the listed size of the exact piece you're shopping for. For round pieces the width is the diameter; add a depth to make it an oval.

Rugs want 12" of floor at the walls (6" is tight). Sizes are checked against the room outline, so fix the outline in Measure first if it's off.

Mark the doors and windows on the walls. Doors show the space they swing or slide through and start the walking routes; windows show whether tall furniture stands in front of them. Pick a door to change its type: swing, double, pocket, barn, sliding or bifold.
