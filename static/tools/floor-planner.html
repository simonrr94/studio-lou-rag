Click a room to select it, drag to move it, drag a corner to resize. For rooms that aren't rectangles, drag a + on a side to add a corner. To remove a corner, tap it and then the × beside it. With no corner picked, Delete removes the room.

Use the listed size of the exact piece you're shopping for. For round pieces the width is the diameter; add a depth to make it an oval.

Rugs want 12" of floor at the walls (6" is tight). Sizes are checked against the room outline, so fix the outline in Measure first if it's off.

Mark the doors that open into a room and the planner shows where they swing, so you can see whether furniture is in the way.
